OUR COMMUNITY

Many disciplines.
One direction.

R&B.AI brings together people who want to shape AI with rigor, generosity and imagination. Anyone who shares our mission is welcome to contribute.

01 · WHO TAKES PART

Our contributors come from machine learning, reinforcement learning, data science, design and beyond. Some are professional researchers, others are students, engineers or simply curious about how AI works.

What they share is a belief that meaningful progress happens in the open, and a wish to help build it.

02 · WAYS TO CONTRIBUTE

Research

Design evaluations, run experiments and share what you find.

Build

Write the code and tools that make our benchmarks and projects work.

Write

Turn methods and results into clear notes and resources for everyone.

Question

Bring a fresh point of view, challenge assumptions and suggest new ideas.

03 · HOW TO JOIN

Three simple steps.

  1. Say hello. Write to contact@rnb-ai.org and tell us a little about yourself and your interests.
  2. Find your place. We will introduce you to the program and working group that fit what you want to do.
  3. Start contributing. Join the work in progress, at your own pace and level of experience.
