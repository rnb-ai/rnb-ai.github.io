ABOUT R&B.AI

Open intelligence,
for everyone.

R&B.AI is an independent non-profit association, founded on 25 July 2026 and registered in France, that promotes, studies and develops artificial intelligence and computer science, in the open.

01 · MISSION STATEMENT

Our mission is to promote, study and develop artificial intelligence and computer science in all their forms, in the open. We share knowledge and foster collaboration, develop open technology projects, raise awareness among the public and professionals of the technological and ethical issues at stake, and offer guidance and support to people and organisations working with AI and digital systems.

02 · WHY WE EXIST

Progress should be shared.

AI is advancing quickly, but the evidence behind that progress is often hard to inspect. Evaluations are not always public, results are hard to reproduce, and the knowledge needed to take part is spread across many places.

R&B.AI exists to build the common ground that AI needs: open evaluations anyone can run, a shared body of knowledge anyone can learn from, and a community where researchers, builders and curious minds can contribute on equal terms.

Everything we produce is published openly, including our code on GitHub. We are independent and act in the general interest, not for profit.

03 · WHAT GUIDES US

Open by default

Our benchmarks, methods and results are published so that anyone can use, check and build on them.

Rigor

We favour careful, reproducible work over quick claims, and we document how results are obtained.

Generosity

Knowledge grows when it is shared. We welcome contributors of every background and level of experience.

Statutory purpose · Article 2 of the statutes (original French text)

Cette association a pour objet de promouvoir, d'étudier et de développer l'intelligence artificielle et l'informatique sous toutes leurs formes. À ce titre, elle se donne pour missions de :

  • Partager des connaissances et favoriser la collaboration dans ces domaines ;
  • Innover en développant des projets technologiques liés à l'IA et au numérique ;
  • Sensibiliser le public et les professionnels aux enjeux technologiques et éthiques ;
  • Proposer des prestations de services, de conseil et d'accompagnement dans les domaines de l'intelligence artificielle, du développement informatique, du codage et des systèmes numériques.
