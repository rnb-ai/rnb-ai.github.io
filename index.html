01 · MISSION STATEMENT

Build the common ground AI needs.

Our mission is to promote, study and develop artificial intelligence and computer science, in the open. R&B.AI is a French non-profit association (loi 1901). We share knowledge and foster collaboration, build open technology projects such as open benchmarks, raise awareness of the technological and ethical issues of AI, and help people and organisations put AI and digital tools to good use.
