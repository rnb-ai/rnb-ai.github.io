OUR PROGRAMS

Practical infrastructure
for accountable AI.

R&B.AI runs three complementary programs. Together they make AI progress easier to measure, easier to understand and open to anyone who wants to take part.

PROGRAM 01

Open benchmarks

Shared evaluations that make progress measurable, reproducible and useful to everyone.

We design, maintain and publish benchmarks for AI systems. Each benchmark comes with its data, its evaluation method and its results, so that anyone can rerun it and compare systems on the same basis.

  • Design of evaluation tasks and datasets
  • Documented, reproducible evaluation protocols
  • Public results that anyone can check

PROGRAM 02

Research commons

A living library of methods, results and questions, available to the whole community.

We gather and share knowledge about how AI systems are built and evaluated: methods, experimental results, open questions and lessons learned. The commons is meant for researchers, students, practitioners and anyone curious about AI.

  • Write-ups of methods and experiments
  • Field notes and open research questions
  • Freely accessible learning resources

PROGRAM 03

Collective action

Collaborative experiments, working groups and real-world tools built in public.

We organise contributors around shared projects. Working groups take on concrete questions, run experiments together and turn the results into open tools that others can reuse.

  • Thematic working groups open to volunteers
  • Collaborative experiments run in public
  • Open-source tools built from shared work
